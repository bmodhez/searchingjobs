Account & Data Deletion Request

At Bharat Jobs (Package: com.bharatjobs.app), we believe you should have complete control over your personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion using either of the following two methods:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Bharat Jobs app installed on your device:

  1. Open the Bharat Jobs app.
  2. Navigate to the Menu tab (for Job Seekers) or Company Profile tab (for Recruiters).
  3. Tap on App Settings.
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account session will be terminated immediately, and all database records will be permanently erased.

Method 2: Email Request (Web / Manual)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can send an email deletion request:

  • Email Address: bhavinmodhh@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Bharat Jobs
  • Body: Please include your registered Phone Number and/or registered Email Address associated with your account.

Our support team will verify your identity and permanently delete your account within 7 business days.

Send Deletion Request

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following records are permanently removed from our active databases:

  • Personal profile data (Full Name, Phone number, Email address, Location, Bio).
  • Uploaded Resumes and Candidate Documents.
  • Active job postings, applicant records, and application history.
  • Saved jobs and notification history.

What Data is Retained?

We do not retain personal identifiable information after account deletion, except strictly necessary financial/tax transaction receipts for recruiter subscription purchases where required by Indian financial and accounting laws.
