Privacy Policy

Application: Bharat Jobs: Local Job Search (Package: com.bharatjobs.app)
Effective Date: October 20, 2026 | Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Summary: Bharat Jobs connects job seekers with verified employers and recruiters across India. We strictly respect your privacy and only collect necessary details to facilitate hiring. We do NOT sell your personal data to any third-party advertisers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bharat Jobs ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (Bharat Jobs, package identifier: com.bharatjobs.app) and related online services.

By downloading, registering, or using Bharat Jobs, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, building a candidate profile, posting job vacancies, or contacting recruiters:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, phone number, email address, profile photo, current city, locality, educational qualifications, job title, and skills.
  • Resume & Employment Documents: Resume files (PDF, DOCX) uploaded by job seekers to apply for openings.
  • Recruiter & Company Information: Company name, registered address, HR manager name, business phone number, company logo, and vacancy specifications.
  • Job Applications & Activity: Information regarding jobs you view, save, apply for, report, or jobs posted and candidates reviewed.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: When recruiters purchase job posting packages or job boosts, payment transactions are processed securely via authorized third-party payment gateways. We do not store full credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.

3. Device Permissions & Sensitive Data

To deliver core location-based job discovery and mobile application features, we request the following permissions:

  • Location (Precise and Approximate Location): Used exclusively to find nearby job vacancies within your city, area, or selected kilometer radius (e.g., jobs within 5km, 10km, or 25km). Location is accessed only while using the app and is never shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Storage & Media Files: Used when you choose to upload a resume file (PDF) or set a profile picture / company logo.
  • Phone & WhatsApp Interaction: Used when a job seeker voluntarily taps "Click to call HR" or "WhatsApp HR" to initiate contact regarding a job posting.
  • Push Notifications: Used to send timely alerts about job application status, recruiter callbacks, urgent job hiring alerts, and important account security notices. You can disable notifications anytime in device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To facilitate job applications and connect job seekers with relevant recruiters.
  • To verify candidate profiles and employer vacancies to prevent spam and fraudulent postings.
  • To calculate distance between job seekers and job locations for commute convenience.
  • To manage recruiter wallets, urgent job boosts, and subscription allowances.
  • To provide customer support and respond directly to user inquiries via email.
  • To comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect users from unauthorized account access.

5. How Information Is Shared

We share information only in the specific scenarios described below:

  • With Recruiters/Employers: When a job seeker applies for a job opening, their candidate profile (Name, Phone number, City, Experience, Resume) is shared with the specific recruiter who posted the job vacancy.
  • With Job Seekers: When a recruiter posts a vacancy, the company details and designated contact method (Phone or WhatsApp) are made visible to qualified job seekers.
  • Service Providers: We employ trusted third-party infrastructure providers to perform platform operations:
    • Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication and verification.
    • Supabase (PostgreSQL Cloud): Encrypted database and storage infrastructure.
    • Expo & Google Play Services: Core mobile runtime, push notification delivery, and app distribution.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable Indian law, court order, or governmental regulations.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

6. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. All database traffic is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL). We retain user profile data for as long as your account is active or needed to provide services.

7. User Rights and Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Access & Edit: You can review and edit your profile details, skills, phone number, and resume at any time within the app settings.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data (profile, job applications, resumes, posted jobs) at any time.

You can request account deletion directly from the mobile app under Settings > Delete Account or by visiting our public Account Deletion Page or emailing us at bhavinmodhh@gmail.com. Upon receiving your verified request, all personal data is purged within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Bharat Jobs is strictly intended for individuals seeking employment or hiring staff who are at least 18 years of age (or the legal working age under applicable laws). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a minor under 13 has provided personal data, we immediately delete it from our servers.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app following any changes signifies your acceptance.
